# Acme Solar Panels

## The 400W Elite Panel
The Acme 400W Elite is our best-selling monocrystalline panel. It has a 22.4% efficiency rating and a power warranty of 25 years. Each panel is rated to produce 400W under standard test conditions, and performs well even in partial shade thanks to its half-cut cell design.

Key specs:
- Power: 400W, efficiency 22.4%
- Warranty: 25-year product and performance warranty
- Dimensions: 1722 x 1134 x 30 mm
- Weight: 21.5 kg
- Operating temperature: -40°C to +85°C
- Certified to IEC 61215 and IEC 61730

## The 550W Max Panel
For bigger rooftops and commercial roofs, the 550W Max delivers the lowest cost per watt. It uses 182 mm silicon M10 cells and ships with a 30-year performance warranty.

Key specs:
- Power: 550W, efficiency 21.8%
- Warranty: 30-year performance warranty
- Dimensions: 2279 x 1134 x 30 mm
- Weight: 27.2 kg
- Certified to IEC 61215 and IEC 61730

## Mounting
We install on sloped tile roofs, flat metal roofs, and ground mounts. All racking is aluminium and stainless steel, warrantied for 25 years. For flat roofs we use a ballasted system with no roof penetrations.
