# Pricing and Financing

## Current list prices (installed, per kWp)
- Residential home system, 3–5 kWp: $2.20 per watt ($2,200 per kWp)
- Large home system, 6–10 kWp: $1.90 per watt
- Small business, up to 20 kWp: $1.60 per watt

A typical 4 kWp home system costs about $8,800 before incentives.

## Financing
We partner with Alma and Klarna for 0% financing over 12 months, and 6.9% APR over 60 months. There is no down payment required for approved customers.

## Incentives
Federal tax credit is 30% of system cost, applied on the buyer's tax return. Some states add local rebates of up to $500. We handle all incentive paperwork at no charge.

## Deposit and timing
A $500 deposit books the installation slot. Once deposit is paid and permits are filed, typical install time is 4–6 weeks. We charge nothing for the site survey.
