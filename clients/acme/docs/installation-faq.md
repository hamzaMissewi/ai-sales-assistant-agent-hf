# Installation FAQ

## How long does installation take?
A typical residential install takes one day. Permits and interconnection take 2–4 weeks before any work starts.

## What if my roof is old?
If the roof is over 15 years old, our surveyor recommends replacing it before installing panels. We can quote the roofing work separately.

## Batteries
We install two battery options:
- Acme Home Battery 10 kWh: $9,500 installed, 10-year warranty.
- Acme Home Battery 13.5 kWh (Tesla Powerwall compatible): $11,900 installed, 10-year warranty.

A battery lets you run essentials during outages and store surplus solar for the evening rate window.

## Maintenance
Panels are virtually maintenance-free. We recommend a simple rinse twice a year. All systems are remotely monitored; the monitoring app is free and included.

## Warranty coverage
The inverter is covered for 12 years. Panels are covered as listed on their spec sheets (25 or 30 years). If a panel or inverter fails, we replace it at no labor cost for the full warranty period.
