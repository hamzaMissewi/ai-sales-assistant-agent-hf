# Financing and Buying Help

## Mortgage pre-approval
Most buyers start with mortgage pre-approval. Banks typically cover 70-80% of the purchase price. We partner with three local banks and can submit pre-approval paperwork at no charge.

## Typical monthly cost example
A $185,000 apartment with a 20% down payment ($37,000) and a 25-year mortgage at 4.2% is about $800/month before insurance.

## Transfer and registration fees
Buyer pays transfer tax of 1.5% of the sale price, plus notary and registration fees of roughly $1,500. Sellers pay the brokerage commission.

## Buying process timeline
1. Pre-approval: 3-5 working days.
2. Offer and signing of preliminary contract: day agreed.
3. Due diligence and title check: 2-3 weeks.
4. Final deed registration and keys: about 30 days from offer.

## Rent-to-own
Two of our developers offer a rent-to-own program: 24-month rental with a fixed purchase option at the end. Ask about current availability.
